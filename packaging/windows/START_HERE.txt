SPIRAL OS: HAKUI ENGINE
WINDOWS STARTUP PACK

Run:
SPIRAL-OS-HAKUI-ENGINE.exe

Controls:
WASD  MOVE
SHIFT SPRINT
SPACE JUMP
E     SIT / STAND / USE ANCHOR
RMB   ORBIT CAMERA
WHEEL ZOOM CAMERA
Q     SWITCH SHOULDER
R     RESET CAMERA
1     ON FOOT
2     SKATEBOARD (VISIBLE RIDE MODE)
3     BMX (VISIBLE RIDE MODE)
4     CAR (DEFERRED / IMMOBILE IN v0.7)
[ / ] LOOK SENSITIVITY
- / + AUDIO VOLUME

FUSION TABLE (ONLY WHILE SEATED):
T     TERMINAL / DICE
G     CARD SUITE
B     BET 25 VIRTUAL CREDITS
H     HIT CARD
J     STAND CARD HAND
I     INSPECT TERMINAL

SPARRING DATUM:
C     ENTER / LEAVE COMBAT
Z     JAB
X     CROSS
V     HOLD GUARD
K     RECOVER AFTER KNOCKDOWN

ESC   PAUSE / RESUME
F10   QUIT

Skateboard and BMX are early embodiment controllers, not full trick systems.
Approaching a seat or spar datum dismounts supported ride modes to ON FOOT.
Virtual credits only. No purchase or cash-out.
